Primary goal
Safe, calm, supervised environment through visible adult presence, controlled access, active circulation, and prompt communication.
Your role
Visibility · support · monitoring. Circulate actively in your assigned zone. Report concerns to the Incident Lead. Stay calm and non-confrontational. Avoid isolated one-on-one interactions. Do not physically intervene unless immediate safety requires it.
Access control
Students check in once — no re-entry. Gates reopen at 3:00 AM for parent pickup. All entrances, exits, fence lines, and parking access areas monitored throughout the event.
⚠ High monitoring areas
Restrooms · Stairwells · Fence corners & perimeter edges · Parking lot boundaries · Staff/storage areas · Kitchenette and service corridors · Back doors — especially the Block Café back door (2nd floor)
Bathroom checks
Same-gender pairs only. Knock and announce before entry. Brief and visibility-oriented. Never a single volunteer alone. You are looking for: illness, vaping, impaired student, conflict, distress.
Communication
Keep it concise and location-based.

"Need support near upstairs restroom."
Not: "There's a problem over here."
Escalate immediately to Incident Lead
Intoxication · Aggression · Medical concern · Emotional distress · Missing student · Unsafe behavior

If a student appears significantly impaired, disoriented, or medically unwell: notify Incident Lead immediately, maintain nearby presence, and do not leave the student unsupervised.
Early removal protocol
Students removed for safety concerns remain supervised until released directly to a parent or guardian. Incident Lead coordinates all early releases.
